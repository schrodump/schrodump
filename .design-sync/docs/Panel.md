The surface. Seven tones, one shape, and the tone carries the meaning: `section` (a card), `info` and `lock` (neutral notes — a lock is a constraint, deliberately not red), `warning` (an amber caution that persists — never a toast), `danger` (what an irreversible action will do) and `error` (a refusal), `empty` (nothing here yet). Pass `role="alert"` for a live warning.
